3. Rental Agreement Process

The general Rental Agreement process is as follows:

  1. The customer selects a FairStay package.
  2. The customer provides the required basic and agreement details.
  3. FairStay prepares the draft based on the information provided.
  4. The customer reviews the draft and identifies any required corrections.
  5. The customer confirms the draft and proceeds with payment.
  6. FairStay confirms receipt of the payment.
  7. After payment confirmation, the confirmed draft is sent for printing and processing.
  8. A scanned copy is sent to the customer's registered email address.
  9. The physical copy is prepared for dispatch, generally on the same evening after processing.
Important: Customers are responsible for carefully reviewing all information in the draft before making the final payment.
